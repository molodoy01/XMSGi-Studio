About the project

Telegram publishing in one clear workspace

XMSGi Studio is a desktop app for composing, scheduling, and sending messages through your Telegram account. It brings together the Studio editor and Quick Planner for quick posts, scheduled delivery, and drafts.

Download XMSGi Studio View the source on GitHub

Who created XMSGi Studio?

XMSGi Studio was created and is maintained by Molodoy, who publishes the project through the @molodoy01 GitHub account. The source code, documentation, and development history are available in the public repository.

Why it was built

The project started with a goal of making Telegram publishing less cumbersome and closer to the way people already work in Telegram. Prepare text and media, review a message, then choose when it should be sent.

The aim is to give publishers more control over their workflow, not to replace Telegram or require a separate service just to keep drafts.

What it helps you do

  • Write and format posts with media and other attachments.
  • Preview messages and send them to selected Telegram chats, groups, and channels through your account.
  • Schedule posts for a specific time or set up recurring messages.
  • Keep drafts, templates, and history; review the queue; edit or cancel scheduled messages.

What makes it different

XMSGi Studio is built around your Telegram account: you do not need to create a separate bot to publish. It is a desktop workspace that brings post composition, Quick Planner, the queue, and drafts together.

Drafts and the working session are stored on your device; XMSGi Studio does not sync drafts through its own cloud. The source code is public under the MIT license, so the implementation can be inspected.
