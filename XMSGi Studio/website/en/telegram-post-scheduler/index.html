Telegram publishing workflow

Schedule Telegram posts from your own account

Write a message, choose a chat and a send time. XMSGi Studio helps you prepare scheduled posts, review the queue, and return to drafts — without a separate Telegram bot.

Download XMSGi Studio Explore the app

How to schedule a Telegram message

  1. 1. Connect your accountSign in to Telegram and choose an available chat, group, or channel.
  2. 2. Prepare your postAdd text, formatting, and attachments, then review the content before sending.
  3. 3. Choose a timeSchedule a one-time post or a repeat and review it in your queue.

Stay in control of your queue

Plans change. Edit, reschedule, or cancel a queued post before it is sent. Your publishing history shows the queue and delivery outcomes, while drafts let you save work and return to it later.

Repeat options include daily, weekly, every two weeks, and monthly schedules, with up to 15 scheduled occurrences.

Your Telegram account, no bot

Posts are sent through your Telegram account. The account session and local drafts stay on your device; message content and attachments are sent to Telegram when you send or schedule a post.

XMSGi Studio is a desktop app for Windows, Linux, and macOS. The Windows build is available as a portable app.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Telegram bot?

No. XMSGi Studio uses your Telegram account to choose chats and publish messages.

Can I schedule posts to channels?

Yes. You can choose a chat, group, or channel available to your account.

Can I edit or cancel a scheduled post?

Yes. Manage a post from the queue before it is sent.
