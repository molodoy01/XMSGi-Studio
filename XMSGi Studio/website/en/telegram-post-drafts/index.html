Preparing posts

Telegram post drafts: save and continue later

Have an idea or a message that is not ready to send? XMSGi Studio lets you keep a working post with its formatting and attachments, then return to it later without choosing a chat or publishing it.

Download XMSGi Studio About scheduled posts

Two ways to keep a draft

  • Quick Planner: the current text, formatting, and attachments are saved automatically. Use Draft to add a separate item to the Planner draft collection.
  • Studio: the current working document is saved automatically. Saved documents can be named, color-labeled, and reopened for editing.

Both collections appear in the shared Posts/History panel. Planner drafts can be selected and moved into Studio, including several at once.

Prepare a post without sending it yet

  1. 1. Write the messageAdd formatting and any attachments in Planner or Studio.
  2. 2. Keep your workLeave the current text to be autosaved or use Draft to create a separate saved item.
  3. 3. Continue laterOpen the draft in Posts/History, edit it, and choose a chat and send time when it is ready.

Where drafts are stored

Drafts and their working data stay on your device: Planner uses local application storage, while Studio uses a local file-based draft store. Drafts are not cloud-synced across devices.

A draft is not a scheduled Telegram message. It will not be sent automatically; you must separately choose to send or schedule it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to choose a Telegram chat to save a draft?

No. You can save a draft without connecting an account or choosing a chat. Those are needed to send or schedule a message.

Are drafts synced with Telegram or other devices?

No. XMSGi Studio drafts are stored locally in the app and are not cloud-synced.

Can I move a draft from Planner to Studio?

Yes. Select saved Planner drafts in Posts/History and move them into Studio to continue working on them.
