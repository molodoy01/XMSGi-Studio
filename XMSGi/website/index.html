How it works

Simple from the first step.

Open XMSGi, pick a Telegram chat, prepare the message, and decide whether it should go now or later.

Your message, your decision
  • Choose an available chat, group, or dialog from your Telegram account.
  • Write the text and add attachments when needed.
  • Send it immediately or set a chosen date and time.
  • Use silent delivery or a message effect when required.
  • Review the outcome in the sending history.

Security and sessions

Your account, your session, your timing.

XMSGi works with a Telegram user account, not a bot. The session is kept locally on the device so you can sign out without losing access for later.

What stays with you
  • Sign out while keeping the Telegram session available for a later return.
  • Forget the account to remove the saved Telegram session from the device.
  • Sensitive local data is protected with Electron safeStorage where the operating system supports it.
